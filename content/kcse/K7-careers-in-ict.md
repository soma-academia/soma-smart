# K7 · Careers in ICT

## Read

### 🧑‍💻 Building systems and software

A computer operator enters data, operates and monitors computers and peripherals, keeps a log of activities and does basic troubleshooting. A programmer writes, tests, debugs, documents and maintains programs. A systems analyst studies the current system, identifies problems, recommends and designs a new system, and writes specifications for programmers. A chief programmer leads the programming team, assigns tasks and makes sure programs meet standards. A software engineer designs, develops and tests system and application software. A computer engineer designs, develops and maintains computer hardware and its components.

### 🌐 Data, networks and the web

A database administrator (DBA) designs and maintains the organisation's databases, controls who can access them, keeps data secure and accurate, and makes backups and restores data after a failure. A network administrator sets up and configures the network, monitors its performance, solves network problems and keeps it secure. A web administrator, or webmaster, develops, tests and updates the organisation's website, monitors traffic and responds to users' queries. A web designer plans and creates the layout and look of web pages. A graphic designer creates images, logos, adverts and other visual material.

### 🛠️ Support, management and business

A computer technician assembles, installs, repairs, upgrades and troubleshoots hardware and software. An ICT trainer or teacher teaches ICT skills, prepares learning materials and assesses learners. An information systems manager plans and controls the ICT department and makes sure the organisation's information needs are met. A data processing manager supervises the data processing section and its staff so that data is processed accurately and on time. A computer sales representative sells hardware and software and advises customers on what to buy. An ICT entrepreneur runs an ICT business, such as a cyber café or a software firm, while an ICT consultant gives expert advice to organisations for a fee.

### 🎓 Further education and choosing a course

After KCSE you can study ICT at public and private universities (degrees), national polytechnics and TVET colleges (certificates and diplomas), or take professional certifications such as ICDL, CCNA or vendor certificates from companies like Microsoft, Cisco and CompTIA. Government-sponsored places are allocated through KUCCPS. When choosing a course or institution, consider: accreditation (universities by the Commission for University Education, CUE, and TVET institutions by the TVET Authority, TVETA), entry requirements (your KCSE grade and subject cluster), cost of fees and upkeep, duration of the course, reputation of the institution, mode of study (full-time, part-time, evening or online), and your own interests and the job market.

### 📝 In the exam

KCSE typically asks: "State three duties of a database administrator", "Differentiate between a programmer and a systems analyst", "Name the ICT career responsible for maintaining a website" or "State three factors to consider when choosing an institution for further training in ICT". Remember the key differences: the systems analyst designs the system, the programmer codes it, the computer operator runs it, the technician repairs it. A computer engineer works on hardware, a software engineer on software. Give duties as action phrases, such as "backing up the database", and give at least as many points as the question asks.

### Task: pick

#### A school's computer lab has several machines that will not boot and one with a broken keyboard. Who is best suited to fix them?
- [ ] A systems analyst
- [x] A computer technician
- [ ] A web designer
- [ ] A data processing manager
Why: Computer technicians assemble, repair, upgrade and troubleshoot hardware and software.

#### A hospital wants to replace its paper patient records with a computerised system. Who should first study the current system and design the new one?
- [ ] A programmer
- [ ] A computer operator
- [x] A systems analyst
- [ ] A graphic designer
Why: The systems analyst studies the existing system, recommends improvements and designs the new system. Programmers then write the code.

#### Wanjiru has a C+ in KCSE and wants an ICT diploma. She finds a college with low fees but cannot confirm it is registered with TVETA. What should she do first?
- [ ] Pay the fees quickly before places run out
- [ ] Choose it because it is the cheapest
- [x] Confirm the college's accreditation before applying
- [ ] Ignore accreditation as long as the course is short
Why: An unaccredited institution may award certificates that employers and other institutions do not recognise. Accreditation comes first.

## Quiz

### Which of these is a duty of a database administrator?
- [ ] Repairing faulty monitors
- [x] Making backups of the database and restoring data after a failure
- [ ] Designing company logos
- [ ] Selling software to customers
Why: A DBA designs and maintains databases, controls access, ensures security and integrity, and backs up and restores data.

### Who studies an existing system, identifies its problems and designs a new system?
- [x] A systems analyst
- [ ] A computer operator
- [ ] A computer technician
- [ ] A web designer
Why: The systems analyst designs the system and writes specifications. The programmer then turns the design into code.

### What is the main difference between a computer engineer and a software engineer?
- [ ] There is no difference
- [ ] A computer engineer only sells computers
- [x] A computer engineer works mainly on hardware; a software engineer works mainly on software
- [ ] A software engineer repairs printers
Why: Computer engineers design and develop hardware and its components. Software engineers design, develop and test system and application software.

### Who leads a team of programmers, assigns tasks and makes sure the programs meet standards?
- [ ] A computer operator
- [ ] A webmaster
- [ ] A data processing manager
- [x] A chief programmer
Why: The chief programmer coordinates the programming team and checks the quality of their work.

### Which ICT professional is responsible for developing, updating and monitoring an organisation's website?
- [ ] A network administrator
- [x] A webmaster (web administrator)
- [ ] A computer technician
- [ ] A database administrator
Why: The webmaster keeps the website running and up to date, monitors traffic and responds to users' queries.

### Which of these is a duty of a computer operator?
- [x] Entering data and keeping a log of computer activities
- [ ] Designing a new information system
- [ ] Writing system specifications
- [ ] Approving the ICT department's budget
Why: Computer operators run the computers day to day: entering data, operating peripherals, keeping logs and doing basic troubleshooting.

### Which body accredits universities in Kenya?
- [ ] KNEC
- [ ] KUCCPS
- [ ] TVETA
- [x] The Commission for University Education (CUE)
Why: CUE accredits universities. TVETA accredits TVET institutions, KUCCPS places students, and KNEC runs national examinations like KCSE.

### Which of these is a professional certification in networking?
- [ ] KCSE
- [x] CCNA
- [ ] A university degree in commerce
- [ ] A driving licence
Why: CCNA (Cisco Certified Network Associate) is a vendor certification for networking. ICDL covers basic computer skills.

### Which of these is NOT a factor to consider when choosing an institution for ICT training?
- [ ] Accreditation of the institution
- [ ] Entry requirements
- [ ] Cost and duration of the course
- [x] The colour of the institution's buildings
Why: Accreditation, entry requirements, cost, duration, reputation and mode of study all affect the value and suitability of the course.

### What does an ICT consultant do?
- [ ] Repairs computers in a cyber café
- [ ] Enters data for a bank
- [x] Gives expert ICT advice to organisations, usually for a fee
- [ ] Teaches ICT in a secondary school
Why: Consultants use their expertise to advise organisations, for example on which system to buy or how to secure their network.
